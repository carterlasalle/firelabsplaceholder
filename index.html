The operational gap

What crews report should be what command sees.

During major incidents, critical updates are often trapped in voice traffic and manual logs. That creates a dangerous delay between what crews report and what command can see.

Phoenix Fire Labs is building MOCKINGBIRD to listen to tactical VHF channels, transcribe traffic in real time, extract locations, units, and incident events, then push structured updates into map-centric command workflows.

Wildfire command intelligence

Phoenix Fire Labs is developing AI-native wildfire command software that transforms live radio communications into structured operational intelligence. MOCKINGBIRD performs real-time speech-to-structure on tactical VHF traffic, extracting location, unit, and event signals and synchronizing them to geospatial command views. By compressing communication-to-visibility latency from hours to seconds, we improve situational awareness, reduce manual cognitive load, and support faster tactical decisions in high-risk wildfire environments.
